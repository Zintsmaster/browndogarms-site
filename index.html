What it does

Everything you need to run PMCS right

No guesswork, no flipping through a binder. Just the right checks, in the right order, for your equipment.

TM-faithful checks

Item Numbers, procedures, and not-mission-capable criteria reproduced verbatim from the equipment's Technical Manual, shown right alongside plain-English steps.

Idiot-proof guided flow

Numbered, tappable steps with engine and setup-state banners and built-in safety warnings — with troubleshooting before you ever mark a fault.

Printable PMCS worksheet

Auto-fills Item Numbers, status symbols, and NMC fault criteria so you can transcribe a clean fault list straight onto your unit's DA Form 5988-E.

Real equipment, real manuals

HMMWV, LMTV, and the MEP-831A/832A tactical quiet generator — each backed by its open-source operator's Technical Manual.

Private & offline

Works entirely offline. Your sessions, operator info, and vehicle profiles stay on your device — nothing is ever uploaded to a server.

How it works

From walk-around to 5988-E

PMCS Guide takes you cleanly from the first check to a ready-to-transcribe fault list.

01

Pick your equipment

Choose your vehicle or generator and set engine and setup state. The app loads the correct checks for it.

02

Work the steps

Tap through each numbered check in plain English, with the verbatim TM procedure and safety warnings right there.

03

Flag faults

Hit a problem? Troubleshoot first, then mark the fault with the correct status symbol and NMC criteria.

04

Print & transcribe

Export a printable worksheet with Item Numbers and faults, ready to copy onto your DA Form 5988-E.

Your data never leaves your phone

PMCS Guide is built to work in the field with no signal. Your sessions, operator details, and vehicle profiles are stored only on your device — there's no account, and your maintenance data is never uploaded.
